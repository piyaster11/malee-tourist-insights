# Russian & Indian Tourist Insights: Thailand

A static Malee mock-up of two of Thailand’s most valuable visitor markets — arrivals, what Russian and Indian travellers search for in their own languages, trip interests, and draft ideas for how Malee could meet them.

**Live:** https://piyaster11.github.io/malee-tourist-insights/

Figures come from public sources (Ministry of Tourism and Sports via ThailandStats and press reports, and others) and are dated and linked on the page. Sample, calculated or draft items are labelled as such. No confidential Malee data is used. This is a mock-up for discussion, not a live data feed and not an approved marketing plan.

GitHub Pages is served from `docs/` on `main`. The page is a single self-contained `docs/index.html`.
