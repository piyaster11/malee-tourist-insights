Monthly seasonality

Russians come overwhelmingly in the cool season: December 2025 was the busiest month (266,213). The quietest was June (73,616). Indians travel more evenly through the year, with their 2025 peak also in December (266,228) and a second bulge around May. Search interest, shown later, follows a similar rhythm.

Phuket — where many of these visitors actually land

Phuket is the centre of gravity for Russians, and a major destination for Indians. Two public counting methods are in use. Airport immigration counts people who clear passport control at Phuket; it misses Russians or Indians who entered in Bangkok and then flew on domestically. TAT visitor figures presented to the province can differ. Do not mix the two series.

Russia — destination interest on Google

Among Russians comparing Phuket, Pattaya and Bangkok on Google, Phuket is the usual leader. Interest rises towards the November–January peak season, in step with arrivals. Bangkok shows an unusual mid-July 2025 spike; Russian arrivals that month were only 81,098, so that spike is much more likely news than a travel wave.

3. Interests and the visitor journey

What they look for, and where Malee could appear, from the first search at home to a bottle in a 7-Eleven on the beach.

Russian visitors

  • Where they go. Phuket is the main gateway (about 1.12 million airport arrivals in 2025). Pattaya and Bangkok also appear in search, but Phuket leads routine destination search.
  • When they come. A sharp November–March peak, quiet mid-year — both in arrivals and in Google interest for «Пхукет» EN: Phuket · TH: ภูเก็ต.
  • How they book. Search language is package-tour heavy: туры, горящие туры EN: tours, last-minute tours · TH: ทัวร์, ทัวร์ราคาพิเศษช่วงนาทีสุดท้าย, hotels, excursions, and flights from Moscow and other cities.
  • Spend. ฿113.95 billion in 2025 receipts, or about ฿60,010 per arrival (calculated from MOTS totals). A high-value market.

Indian visitors

  • What attracts them. TAT analysis reported in The Nation, 13 Aug 2026: Thai food is the leading attraction, then beaches, then spa and wellness. TAT’s earlier SATTE 2025 briefing also listed beach, cuisine, nightlife, wellness and heritage, with Bangkok, Chon Buri, Phuket, Krabi and Surat Thani as top destinations (TAT Newsroom, 20 Feb 2025).
  • Who they are. Same Aug 2026 briefing: 68.63% free independent travellers, 31.37% tour groups; 59.68% first-time visitors. Average stay about 7.17 days; published spend ฿6,203 per day or about ฿36,354 per trip.
  • Weddings. Wedding and celebration travel is a TAT priority, especially November–March. The tourism minister argued that a 30-day visa-free stay is needed for Indian wedding groups (same article, 13 Aug 2026).
  • Vegetarian food. Dietary needs, including vegetarian meals, are a known requirement for Indian wedding groups and many leisure visitors. Treat specific menu-gap statistics as something to confirm with hotels and operators — not invented here.

Middle East — later phase

  • Champ also liked opening this work to Middle East travellers later. This mock-up does not yet include that market’s arrival or search data.
  • Any later brief should add Arabic (and English) search terms, and a clear halal note on ingredients, certification and where products can be sold or sampled.
  • Phuket already has new Middle East air connectivity in the 2026 high season (for example Kuwait Airways Kuwait–Phuket, reported by The Phuket News, 29 Sep 2026).

4. How Malee can capture it

All of the items below are proposals for discussion with Marketing, Commercial and Operations. None is an approved campaign.

SEO — be findable in their language

  • Russian landing pages around Phuket, “what to try in Thailand”, coconut water and fruit, with the queries above in the copy.
  • English and Hindi pages for “Thailand trip”, Phuket itineraries, coconut water and vegetarian-friendly drinks.
  • Google Maps / store finder so a visitor can type “Malee Coco” or «кокосовая вода» EN: coconut water · TH: น้ำมะพร้าว near Patong, Kata or Suvarnabhumi and see a nearby shop.
  • For Russia, also register and optimise on Yandex (Webmaster and Business), not only Google.

SEM — paid search ideas

  • Russia: Yandex Direct plus Google Ads, bid on destination + hotel/tour terms only where the ad is honestly about a drink they can buy in Thailand — not on competing with tour operators for “туры на пхукет” EN: tours to Phuket · TH: ทัวร์ไปภูเก็ต unless there is a travel partner. Safer: coconut water, what to try, airport, fruit, and brand terms.
  • India: Google Ads on Thailand trip / Phuket things to do (content and store-locator ads), coconut water / नारियल पानी EN: coconut water · TH: น้ำมะพร้าว (product), and honeymoon/wedding drink occasions.
  • Pull real volumes and suggested bids from Keyword Planner and Wordstat before any budget is set.

Social and platforms

  • Russians: Telegram, VK, Yandex and YouTube — short “first day in Phuket” and “what to drink in the heat” films in Russian.
  • Indians: Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp, as TAT already uses, plus Reels around food, beaches and wedding/honeymoon tables.
  • No follower or view targets are shown here; those need a media plan.

Airport and on-ground activations

  • Airports: sampling of Malee Coco at Phuket (HKT) — the main Russian gateway — and Suvarnabhumi (BKK), where many Indians arrive. Peak staffing in November–March, plus Indian holiday and wedding waves.
  • Hotels and wedding venues: welcome drinks and banquet juice lists, with vegetarian-friendly options clearly marked.
  • Convenience and beach: 7-Eleven and tourist-strip cold boxes; simple Russian and Hindi talkers.
  • Tour operators: include a bottle or voucher in Phuket excursion packs, especially Russian-language desks.
